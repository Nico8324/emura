Privacy Policy · updated 8 October 2026

We collect nothing

Emura collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us. Your games, saves, save states, settings and play time stay on your device, and, if you support Emura, in your own iCloud account.

iCloud

If you support Emura, your saves, save states, profiles and console settings (owner, characters, coins) sync through your own iCloud account, so your other devices have them too. They're stored in your iCloud storage, private to your Apple Account: we can't see them. Your games themselves are never uploaded.

Sync happens when you open Emura and when a game ends. When it replaces or removes a save, Emura keeps the previous copy on your device for 30 days. Signing out of iCloud leaves every save on your device. You can delete Emura's data from your iCloud storage in your device's iCloud settings.

Game artwork

To show your games' boxes and screens, Emura asks for them online. These requests carry only what's needed to find the picture: never your games themselves, your saves or anything about you.

  • Boxes, title screens and screenshots come from libretro-thumbnails. Emura asks for them by the game's name and region.
  • SteamGridDB, only if you add your own SteamGridDB key in Settings › Artwork: Emura asks for backgrounds and logos by the game's name, with your key. Your key stays in your iCloud Keychain and is sent only to SteamGridDB. SteamGridDB's privacy policy applies to it.

Like any website you visit, these services see your device's internet address when Emura asks them for a picture. Game details and descriptions are inside the app: looking them up sends nothing.

Permissions

Emura asks for a permission only when a game needs it, and uses it only on your device.

  • Motion & Fitness: your step count, turned into the coins some 3DS games use.
  • Camera: while a game uses the console's camera, for example to scan a QR code. Pictures are not stored or sent.
  • Microphone: while a game listens, when you blow or speak. Sound is not recorded or sent.
  • Photos (add only): to save your screenshots and the photos games take to your library. Emura cannot see your photos.
  • Notifications: reminders made on your device, such as the one before a free trial ends.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

Children

Emura collects no data from anyone, children included.

When you contact us

An email to us is kept only to answer it. Reports and discussions on GitHub are public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement: don't put anything private in them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will say so before that version ships.
