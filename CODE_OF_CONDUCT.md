# Be kind

Emura's issues and discussions are for helping each other play.

- Be respectful and patient. Everyone was new once.
- Stay on topic: Emura, its games and how they run.
- **No piracy.** Never share or ask for game files, BIOS files, keys or links to them. Posts that do are deleted, and repeat offenders are blocked.
- No harassment, hate or spam.

Report anything that breaks these rules to emura.support@icloud.com.
